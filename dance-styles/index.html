Frequently Asked Questions

How many dance styles does Danceify support?

Danceify offers 17 dance styles: EDM Shuffle, Salsa, TikTok, Bollywood, Samba, Greek, Hip-hop, Ballet, Tango, Flamenco, Reggaeton, Swing, Belly Dance, Hula, Country Line, K-Pop and Irish. There are also Premium themed templates.

Which dance style works best for a pet photo?

All 17 styles work with pet photos — dogs and cats perform the choreography on their hind legs. The most visually striking pet results tend to come from Hip-Hop, K-Pop, Salsa, and Flamenco, where the movement is bold enough to read clearly. Hula and Ballet work especially well for cats. For tips on photo selection, see the FAQ.

How do I pick the right dance style for my video?

Think about mood and audience: festive and celebratory — try Samba or Bollywood. Dramatic and intense — go with Tango or Flamenco. Social media and viral — TikTok Viral or K-Pop. Folk and heritage — Irish Dance, Greek Dance, or Country Line. You can always try more than one style with the same photo in Danceify.
