About EDM Shuffle

EDM Shuffle is a fast footwork dance done to electronic dance music. The feet carry the dance with quick heel-and-toe steps, slides and kicks that land on the beat, while the arms and upper body stay loose.

Most routines are built from a small set of steps. In the running man, one knee lifts while the standing foot slides back, then the feet switch. In the T-step, the standing foot swivels in and out while the other foot kicks out to the side. Dancers string these steps together and add spins and kicks to fit the music.
