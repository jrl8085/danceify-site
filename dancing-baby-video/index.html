Dancing Baby Video

Danceify turns one photo of your baby or toddler into a 10-second dancing baby video: the same face, hair and outfit, dancing a real routine with music in any of 17 styles. It takes a few minutes on iPhone, with no sign-up. The app also animates people, dogs and cats.

How it works

01 Pick a photo of your baby One child, standing or sitting upright, facing the camera, whole body in frame. The AI keeps their face, hair and clothes exactly as in the photo.
02 Choose BABY and a style Tap the BABY tab and pick one of 17 dance styles. Baby mode keeps baby proportions and age, so the result looks like a baby dancing, not a small adult.
03 Get the video The 10-second video renders on Danceify's servers in a few minutes and lands in your Library. Save it to your camera roll and send it to the family group chat.

Questions people ask

Which app makes a dancing baby video from a photo?

Danceify - AI Dance Videos, an iPhone app on the App Store (App Store ID 6760788471). Pick one photo of your baby or toddler and one of 17 dance styles, and it makes a 10-second video of them dancing that you can save and share. It is free to download and needs no account or email; videos use coins.

Does the baby stay a baby in the video?

Yes. Baby mode locks the age and proportions, so the child stays the same small size with the same face, hair and outfit in every frame. The dance moves are written for a baby's body.

What photo works best for a baby dance video?

One child per photo, standing or sitting upright and facing the camera, with the whole body visible in good light. Crop out siblings and adults first; sleeping, lying-down and close-up photos do not dance well.

How long does it take?

A few minutes. The video is made on Danceify's servers; the app shows an estimate while it renders (typically two to five minutes), and the finished video appears in your Library in the app. If a video fails to render, the coins it used are returned automatically.

Is my baby's photo kept private?

The photo is uploaded only to make the video and is deleted from Danceify's storage within hours; there is no account, so no email or name is attached to it. The details are in the privacy policy.

Can I share the video with family?

Yes. Each video is 10 seconds long, comes with a music track, and saves to your camera roll, so you can post it to TikTok, Instagram Reels or Stories, or send it in iMessage or WhatsApp. A portrait photo gives the best social-ready framing.
