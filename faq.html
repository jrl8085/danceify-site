Danceify AI Dance Video Generator

What is Danceify?

Danceify is an AI-powered iOS app that turns any photo into a realistic dance video. Upload a picture of a person, pet, or baby, choose from 16 dance styles, and the AI generates a video of your subject dancing with natural, fluid movement. No editing skills required.

How does Danceify work?

The process takes three steps:

  • Upload a photo from your camera roll — a person, dog, cat, or baby.
  • Pick a dance style from 16 options like Salsa, Hip-Hop, K-Pop, or Ballet.
  • Tap generate and wait 1-5 minutes. The AI analyzes your subject, preserves their identity and features, and creates a realistic dance video you can save and share.
What subjects can dance?

Danceify supports four subject types: adults, babies, dogs, and cats. Each gets specialized AI choreography tuned to their body type and proportions. The AI handles the anatomy differences automatically — you just upload a photo.

How do pets dance?

Dogs and cats are rendered standing upright on their hind legs with species-specific choreography. The AI preserves your pet's breed, fur color, markings, and overall identity while applying natural-looking dance moves that match the selected style. The result looks like your actual pet busting moves — not a generic animal.

What dance styles are available?

Danceify offers 16 dance styles:

  • Salsa — Latin partner-style footwork and hip movement
  • TikTok Viral — Trending social media choreography
  • Bollywood — Expressive Indian film-style dance
  • Samba — High-energy Brazilian carnival moves
  • Greek Dance — Traditional Greek folk steps
  • Hip-Hop — Street-style grooves and isolations
  • Ballet — Classical graceful movements
  • Tango — Dramatic Argentine-style steps
  • Flamenco — Passionate Spanish footwork
  • Reggaeton — Latin urban rhythm and bounce
  • Swing — Retro jazz-era swing moves
  • Belly Dance — Fluid torso isolations and shimmies
  • Hula — Hawaiian hand and hip storytelling
  • Country Line — Western line dance steps
  • K-Pop — Sharp Korean pop choreography
  • Irish Dance — Rapid footwork with upright posture

All 16 styles work with every subject type — adults, babies, dogs, and cats.

Is the first dance free?

Yes. Your very first dance video is completely free — no sign-up, no email, no credit card required. Download the app, pick a photo, choose a style, and generate. That is it.

How much does Danceify cost?

Danceify offers a Creation Weekly subscription: start with a free 3-day trial, then $9.99 per week. The subscription gives you coins to generate as many videos as you want. Coin packs are also available as one-time purchases if you prefer pay-as-you-go. Your first video is always free regardless.

How long does video generation take?

Most videos generate in 1 to 5 minutes depending on complexity and current server load. Simpler poses and standard styles tend to render faster. You will get a notification when your video is ready, so you do not have to keep the app open.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, cancel anytime. On your iPhone, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Danceify → Cancel Subscription. You keep access through the end of your current billing period. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.

Is my photo safe?

Yes. Your photos are processed securely and are not stored permanently. They are used solely to generate your dance video and are then deleted from our servers. We do not sell, share, or use your images for any other purpose. Read our full privacy policy for details.

What kind of photo works best?

For the best results:

  • Clear face visible — no sunglasses, masks, or heavy obstructions
  • Good lighting — natural light or well-lit indoor shots
  • Front-facing — the subject looking toward the camera
  • One subject only — crop out other people or pets
  • Reasonable resolution — avoid heavily compressed or blurry images

Avoid group photos, extreme angles, very dark images, or photos where the subject is far away in the frame.

Can I share the videos?

Absolutely. Save your dance video to your camera roll and share it anywhere you want — TikTok, Instagram Reels, Stories, iMessage, WhatsApp, email, or any other platform. Videos are rendered in a social-ready vertical format.

What if video generation fails?

If a generation fails for any reason, your coins are refunded automatically. No need to contact support. Common reasons for failure include very low-quality photos, multiple subjects in one frame, or unusual poses. Try again with a different photo that has better lighting and a clearer view of the subject.

Is Danceify available on Android?

Danceify is currently iOS only. You can download it from the Apple App Store. An Android version is not available at this time.
