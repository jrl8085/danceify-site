What is Danceify?

Danceify - AI Dance Videos is an iPhone app on the App Store (App Store ID 6760788471). It turns one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat into a 10-second dance video, in any of 17 dance styles, and there are also Premium themed templates.

How does Danceify work?

Pick one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat, choose one of 17 dance styles or a Premium themed template, and Danceify makes a 10-second dance video that you can save to your phone and share.

The process takes three steps:

  • Upload a photo from your camera roll — a person, dog, cat, or baby.
  • Pick a dance style from 17 options like EDM Shuffle, Salsa, Hip-hop, K-Pop or Ballet.
  • Get your video: a 10-second dance video you can save to your phone and share.
What subjects can dance?

Danceify supports four subject types: adults (photo to dance video), babies (dancing baby video), dogs (make my dog dance video), and cats (AI cat dance). Each gets specialized AI choreography tuned to their body type and proportions. The AI handles the anatomy differences automatically — you just upload a photo.

How do pets dance?

Dogs and cats are rendered standing upright on their hind legs with species-specific choreography. The AI preserves your pet's breed, fur color, markings, and overall identity while applying natural-looking dance moves that match the selected style. The result looks like your actual pet busting moves — not a generic animal. See make my dog dance video and AI cat dance.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, cancel anytime. On your iPhone, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Danceify → Cancel Subscription. You keep access through the end of your current billing period. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is uploaded to our cloud storage and sent to the AI services that generate your video. We use it only for that, and we never sell it or use it for advertising. We delete it from our storage when your video is done, and in any case within hours. Our privacy policy names the services involved and says how long each kind of data is kept.

What kind of photo works best?

For the best results:

  • Clear face visible — no sunglasses, masks, or heavy obstructions
  • Good lighting — natural light or well-lit indoor shots
  • Front-facing — the subject looking toward the camera
  • One subject only — crop out other people or pets
  • Reasonable resolution — avoid heavily compressed or blurry images

Avoid group photos, extreme angles, very dark images, or photos where the subject is far away in the frame.

Can I share the videos?

Absolutely. Save your dance video to your camera roll and share it anywhere you want — TikTok, Instagram Reels, Stories, iMessage, WhatsApp, email, or any other platform. Videos are rendered in a social-ready vertical format.

What if video generation fails?

If a generation fails, your coins are refunded automatically. If that does not happen, email us at danceify.app@gmail.com. Common reasons for failure include very low-quality photos, multiple subjects in one frame, or unusual poses. Try again with a different photo that has better lighting and a clearer view of the subject.
