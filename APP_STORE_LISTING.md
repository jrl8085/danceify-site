## Description
Make ANYONE dance — even your dog. Even your baby. Powered by AI.

Danceify takes a single photo and turns the subject into a real dancer. Pick a dog photo and your dog stands up on its hind legs and dances like a person — the way only the best viral pet videos do, except it's yours, in seconds. Pick a baby and watch them bob and clap to the beat. Pick yourself, your friend, your grandma — and watch them pull off K-Pop choreography you'd never attempt in real life.

WHY DANCEIFY IS DIFFERENT
- Dog mode: a tuned AI pipeline that puts your dog upright on two legs and choreographs real dance moves — not the same trick every other "AI pet" app does.
- Baby mode: keeps your baby a baby (no aging into a creepy adult dancer). Bouncing, clapping, head bobs, joyful baby energy.
- Adult mode: full-body choreography with sharp, style-accurate moves.
- The AI uses the right anatomy and movement vocabulary for each subject — front paws move like paws, baby limbs stay baby-sized, adult bodies do adult dancing.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick a photo from your library — or snap a new one.
2. Choose the subject — Adult, Baby, or Dog.
3. Choose a dance style — 16 to pick from: K-Pop, Salsa, Irish Dance, TikTok Viral, Bollywood, Samba, Greek, Hip-Hop, Ballet, Tango, Flamenco, Reggaeton, Swing, Belly Dance, Hula, Country Line.
4. AI generates your video in 1–3 minutes.
5. Score with music, set the tempo, save to your library, share anywhere.
