How it works

01 Pick a photo One subject, facing the camera, whole body in frame. The AI keeps the face, clothes, fur and markings from the photo.
02 Choose who is dancing and a style PERSON, BABY, DOG or CAT, then one of 17 dance styles. Pets are stood up on their hind legs first; each style has its own music.
03 Get the video The 10-second video renders on Danceify's servers in a few minutes and lands in your Library. Save it to your camera roll and share it anywhere.

Questions people ask

What is Danceify?

Danceify - AI Dance Videos is an iPhone app on the App Store (App Store ID 6760788471). It turns one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat into a 10-second dance video, in any of 17 dance styles, and there are also Premium themed templates.

Who is it for?

Dog and cat owners who want to see their pet dance, parents making a dancing baby video for the family, and anyone who wants to turn a photo of themselves or a friend into a TikTok-style dance clip. No editing skills needed.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, email or password. Install the app and start with a photo.

How much does it cost?

Danceify is free to download, with no account, sign-up or email; purchases go through your Apple Account. Videos use coins (◆): one-time coin packs start at $2.99 (US), and Creation Weekly is $9.99 a week in the US with 3,000 coins a week, plus an introductory offer for new subscribers whose price and length the App Store shows before you confirm. The App Store shows the price for your country.

How long does a video take?

A few minutes. The video is made on Danceify's servers; the app shows an estimate while it renders (typically two to five minutes), and the finished video appears in your Library in the app. If a video fails to render, the coins it used are returned automatically.

Which phones does it run on?

Danceify is an iPhone app on the App Store. There is no Android or web version. Download it from the App Store.

What dance styles are there?

Seventeen today: EDM Shuffle, Salsa, TikTok, Bollywood, Samba, Greek, Hip-hop, Ballet, Tango, Flamenco, Reggaeton, Swing, Belly Dance, Hula, Country Line, K-Pop and Irish. Each style has choreography written separately for people, babies, dogs and cats, and its own music tracks.

Are there Premium templates?

Yes. Besides the 17 dance styles, Danceify has Premium themed templates.

What language is the app in?

English.

How do I find the right app on the App Store?

Its App Store name is Danceify - AI Dance Videos and its App Store ID is 6760788471 (https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760788471). If an App Store page shows a different ID, it is a different app.
