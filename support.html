Frequently Asked

How do I get more dances?

Everyone gets 1 free dance video to start. After that, you can subscribe to Plus ($6.99/week, 20 dances/week, 7-day free trial) or Pro ($13.99/week, 35 dances/week, includes priority rendering and commercial use rights). You can also purchase credit packs (5/15/40 credits) from the "Buy Credits" tab — 1 credit = 1 dance, and credit packs never expire. Existing subscribers on legacy monthly or yearly plans (Lite, Pro Monthly, Pro Yearly, Creator) keep their plans without changes.

How long does a video take to generate?

Most videos are ready in 1–3 minutes. Generation time depends on server load.

Do purchased credits expire?

No. Credit packs you buy are yours forever. Subscription credits replenish monthly.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. Find Danceify and tap Cancel. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

How do I restore purchases?

In the app, go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". This will recover any subscriptions or credit packs tied to your Apple ID.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Sign-up is optional in Danceify — most users are anonymous and identified only by a random app-generated ID. To request deletion, email us at danceify.app@gmail.com. For email-signed-in users, send the request from the account email. For anonymous users, include the app-generated user ID shown in the app's Settings page.
