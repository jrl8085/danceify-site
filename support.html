Frequently Asked

How do I get dances?

Every dance is fueled by coins. You can earn coins with a subscription (weekly, monthly, or yearly), or buy them one-time as a coin pack that never expires.

Weekly subscriptions: Starter ($4.99/wk), Creation ($9.99/wk — 3-day free trial), Advanced ($14.99/wk), Elite ($19.99/wk) or Prestige ($69.99/wk). Higher tiers grant more coins per week.

Monthly & yearly subscriptions: Creation Monthly ($29.99/mo, ~12,000 coins/month) or Creation Yearly ($149.99/yr, ~62,400 coins/year — best long-term value).

One-time coin packs: A $2.99 Starter Pack is available once per Apple ID, plus refill packs at $2.99, $7.99, $17.99, $39.99, and $99.99. None of them expire.

Existing subscribers on legacy plans (Plus, Pro Weekly, Pro Monthly, Pro Yearly, Lite, Creator) keep their plans without changes.

How long does a video take to generate?

Most videos are ready in 2 to 7 minutes. Generation time depends on server load. Keep Danceify open while your video is being made.

Do purchased coins expire?

No. Coins from one-time packs are yours forever. Subscription coins are refilled every week on every plan, including the monthly and yearly plans. Coins that come with a free trial are for the trial: in app version 5.25 and later, any you have not used expire when the trial ends, unless your paid subscription starts.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. Find Danceify and tap Cancel. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

How do I restore purchases?

In the app, go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". This restores an active subscription tied to your Apple Account. If coins you bought are missing afterwards, email us with the user ID shown in Settings.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. In the app, Settings → Delete Account removes your account, your coin history and your Premium dances. It does not cancel a subscription; cancel that in your iPhone's Settings first. To delete everything else we hold, including uploaded photos and analytics data, or to ask for a copy of your data, email us at danceify.app@gmail.com with the user ID shown in the app's Settings page (or write from your account email if you signed in with one). The privacy policy has the details.
