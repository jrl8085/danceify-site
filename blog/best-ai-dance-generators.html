The 5 Tools, Reviewed

01 Danceify

Best for: iOS users who want quick, style-specific dance videos with pet support.

Danceify is a native iOS app that focuses exclusively on turning photos into dance videos. It offers 16 pre-built dance styles — from Salsa and Hip-Hop to K-Pop and Irish Dance — each with distinct choreography created for that genre. You do not write prompts or describe movements. You pick a style, and the AI handles the rest.

The standout feature is subject variety. Danceify supports adults, babies, dogs, and cats. Pet videos show the animal standing on its hind legs with species-specific choreography, and the AI preserves breed-specific features like fur markings, ear shape, and coloring. It is the most polished pet dance experience we tested.

Identity preservation is strong across the board. The output looks like the actual person (or pet) in your photo dancing — not a generic character with a face swap. The first video is free with no sign-up required, which makes it easy to test quality before paying anything.

The main limitation is platform: it is iOS only. There is no web version and no Android app. If you are on an iPhone, it is the most streamlined option available. If you are not, you will need to look elsewhere.

02 Viggle AI

Best for: Custom motion control and character animation on web.

Viggle AI is a web-based tool that lets you animate characters using motion references. Rather than picking from a fixed set of dance styles, you can provide reference videos or describe the motion you want. This gives you more creative control at the cost of more complexity.

The platform started on Discord and has expanded to a web interface. Output quality is competitive, particularly for human subjects. The motion transfer technology handles complex movements well, though results can be inconsistent with unusual reference videos.

Pet support is limited compared to dedicated pet dance tools. Viggle works best with human subjects. The free tier provides a reasonable number of generations for testing, with credit packs available for heavier usage.

Pricing: Free tier with limited credits. Paid credits for additional generations.

03 Kling AI

Best for: High-quality motion generation with fine control.

Kling AI is developed by Kuaishou and offers advanced video generation capabilities through a web interface. It produces high-quality motion output and supports both image-to-video and text-to-video workflows. The motion quality is among the best available, with natural-looking body mechanics and fluid transitions.

For dance specifically, Kling works through its image-to-video pipeline where you provide a source image and prompt describing the desired movement. This requires more manual input compared to apps with pre-built dance styles, but offers more creative flexibility. Results are generally high quality, though generation times can be longer.

The platform provides free credits for new users and offers subscription plans for regular use. Pet dancing is possible but requires careful prompting and produces variable results compared to tools built specifically for animal subjects.

Pricing: Free credits for new accounts. Subscription plans for regular use.

04 Gaga AI

Best for: Text-to-motion with emotion and expression control.

Gaga AI takes a different approach by focusing on text-driven motion generation with emotional expression control. You describe the movement you want, and the AI generates it. This includes the ability to specify mood, energy level, and emotional tone — not just the physical movements.

The output quality is solid for human subjects, with good facial expression rendering and body movement. The text-to-motion approach means you are not limited to pre-set dance styles — you can describe any movement sequence you imagine. However, this also means you need to know what to ask for, which can be a barrier for casual users who just want to see their friend do the salsa.

Pet support is not a focus. Gaga AI is built primarily for human character animation. If you need dog or cat dance videos, this is not the right tool.

Pricing: Free tier with limited generations. Credit-based pricing for additional use.

05 AI Baby Dancer

Best for: Niche baby dance content.

AI Baby Dancer does exactly what the name suggests — it creates dance videos specifically featuring babies. The app is narrowly focused on this single use case, which means it handles baby proportions and movements well but does not extend to other subject types or broader dance style libraries.

The style selection is more limited than general-purpose tools, and the output is geared toward cute, shareable social media content rather than realistic choreography. If your primary goal is making baby dance videos for family and friends, this tool serves that niche. For anything beyond that scope, a more versatile tool will serve you better.

No pet support. No adult subject support. This is a single-purpose tool.

Pricing: Limited free tier. In-app purchases for additional generations.

How to Choose

The right tool depends on what you actually need:

  • Want a polished iOS app with pet support? Danceify is the clear choice. 16 built-in styles, dogs and cats, no prompt writing. First video free.
  • Want custom motion control on the web? Viggle AI gives you the most creative flexibility through reference-based motion transfer.
  • Want the highest quality motion output? Kling AI produces some of the most natural-looking movement, but requires more manual input.
  • Want text-driven emotion control? Gaga AI lets you describe mood and energy alongside movements.
  • Only need baby dance videos? AI Baby Dancer handles that niche well but nothing beyond it.

Key Trends in AI Dance Video Generation

The AI dance video space has matured considerably through 2026. A few trends stand out:

  • Identity preservation has improved dramatically. Early AI dance tools often produced generic-looking faces or obvious deepfake artifacts. Current tools — particularly Danceify and Kling — maintain the subject's actual appearance with high fidelity.
  • Pet support is a real differentiator. Most tools still focus primarily on human subjects. Danceify's investment in species-specific choreography for dogs and cats gives it a unique advantage in the pet content space.
  • Pre-built styles vs. custom prompts represents a genuine UX trade-off. Apps like Danceify that offer curated styles provide consistency and ease of use. Tools like Viggle and Gaga that accept custom input offer more flexibility but higher friction.
  • Mobile-native apps deliver a faster experience. Web-based tools are more accessible across platforms, but native apps like Danceify provide a more streamlined workflow for phone-centric use cases like social media content creation.
