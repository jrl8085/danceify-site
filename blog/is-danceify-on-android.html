Is Danceify on Android?

Platforms · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

No. As of November 10, 2026, Danceify - AI Dance Videos is an iPhone and iPad app, available only on the Apple App Store (ID 6760788471). There is no Android version and no web version.

Key facts
  • Name on the App Store: Danceify - AI Dance Videos. App Store ID 6760788471.
  • Runs on: iPhone and iPad (iOS).
  • Not on: Android, Google Play, the web, Mac or Windows.
  • What it does: turns one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat into a 10-second dance video in 17 styles.
  • Price: free to download; videos use coins. Coin packs from $3.99 (US); Creation Weekly $11.99 a week (US) with 4,000 coins. No free trial.
  • App languages: English, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Korean, German, French, Italian.

People ask this question in several forms: "Danceify APK", "Danceify for Android", "Danceify Google Play", "Danceify online". The answer to all of them is the same. The app exists for iPhone and iPad and nowhere else. This page explains what that means in practice and what you can do if you or the person you want to share with has an Android phone.

Why is there no Android version?

Danceify is built for iOS. It is sold through the Apple App Store, and purchases, subscriptions and refunds go through your Apple Account. There is no Google Play listing, no APK to download, and no plan we can announce for one. If that changes, this page will say so; the date at the top tells you when it was last checked.

Any file called a "Danceify APK" on a download site is not from us. We do not publish an Android app, so we cannot say what such a file contains. Do not install it.

Is there a web version I can use on any device?

No. Danceify does not make videos in a browser. This website explains the app; it does not run it. There is also no Mac or Windows app. The only way to make a Danceify video is the iPhone or iPad app.

What can I do if I have an Android phone?

You have two practical options.

01 Use an iPhone or iPad you have access to

The app runs on iPad as well as iPhone, so a family iPad works. Download Danceify from the App Store on that device, pick the photo (you can send it to the iPhone or iPad first by email, WhatsApp or a shared album), make the video, and save it. The video is a normal video file. No account is needed, so nothing ties the video to that device.

02 Have someone with an iPhone make it and send it to you

Finished videos save to the phone's camera roll and can be shared like any other video: Messages, WhatsApp, email, TikTok, Instagram. The person who receives it does not need the app. Android phones play the video without any extra step.

Can I share a Danceify video with Android friends?

Yes. A Danceify video is a 10-second vertical video saved to your iPhone. Send it in a group chat, post it to TikTok or Instagram, or attach it to an email. It plays on Android, on a computer, and on a TV. Nobody who receives it needs Danceify or an iPhone.

What does the iPhone app do?

Danceify turns one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat into a 10-second dance video. There are 17 dance styles (EDM Shuffle, Salsa, TikTok, Bollywood, Samba, Greek, Hip-hop, Ballet, Tango, Flamenco, Reggaeton, Swing, Belly Dance, Hula, Country Line, K-Pop, Irish) plus Premium themed templates. You pick a track in the app and can change it on the player. The app follows the iPhone's language and is available in English, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Korean, German, French and Italian.

It is free to download, with no account, sign-up or email. Videos use coins (◆): one-time coin packs start at $3.99 (US), and Creation Weekly is $11.99 a week in the US with 4,000 coins a week; eligible new subscribers may get the first week for $2.99 (US) with 1,200 coins. A Regular video uses 250 coins in app version 5.38 and later (150 coins in earlier versions). The App Store shows the price for your country. For the full breakdown, see Danceify pricing explained.
