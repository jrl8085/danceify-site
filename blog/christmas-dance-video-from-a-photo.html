Christmas Dance Video From a Photo

Holiday guide · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer

Danceify - AI Dance Videos, an iPhone app, turns one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat into a 10-second dance video you can send as a holiday card or drop into the family group chat. As of November 10, 2026, there are 17 dance styles and no Christmas-specific template: the festive part comes from your photo, the dance from the style you pick.

Key facts
  • App: Danceify - AI Dance Videos, App Store ID 6760788471. iPhone and iPad only.
  • What you get: a 10-second vertical video from one photo, saved to your phone, shareable anywhere.
  • Holiday template: none. 17 dance styles plus Premium themed templates; none is Christmas-specific.
  • Who can dance: a person, a baby, a dog or a cat. One subject per video.
  • Time: a few minutes per video.

Every December the same photo gets sent around: the family in matching sweaters, the dog in antlers, the baby by the tree. Danceify turns one of those photos into a short dance clip. This guide is honest about what the app does and does not do for the holidays, which styles fit, how to handle pets and babies, what it costs, and what happens to the photo you upload.

Is there a Christmas template in Danceify?

No. Danceify has 17 Regular dance styles: EDM Shuffle, Salsa, TikTok, Bollywood, Samba, Greek, Hip-hop, Ballet, Tango, Flamenco, Reggaeton, Swing, Belly Dance, Hula, Country Line, K-Pop and Irish, plus Premium themed templates. None of them is a Christmas, Hanukkah or New Year template, and the app does not add snow, a tree or a Santa hat. We would rather say that plainly than have you expect something the app does not do.

What the app does do is keep what is in your photo: the sweater, the tree behind you, the hat on the dog. Pick a photo that already looks like the holidays and the video will too.

How do I make it?

01 Download Danceify

Get Danceify - AI Dance Videos from the App Store. Free to download; no account, sign-up or email.

02 Choose who is dancing

On Home, tap ADULT, BABY, DOG or CAT. One subject per video.

03 Pick a style and a photo

Choose a dance style from the table above. Then pick one photo from your camera roll: one person or pet, whole body visible, good light, facing the camera. Pick a track in the MUSIC row.

04 Tap CREATE, then save and send

Danceify makes a 10-second video. You can change the track on the player. Save it to your camera roll and send it by Messages, WhatsApp or email, or post it to Instagram or TikTok.

Can the dog, the cat or the baby dance?

Yes, and these are the videos families send most. Use the DOG, CAT or BABY tab, and one photo where the whole body is visible. A dog or cat can be sitting or standing in the photo; the app stands them up for the dance and keeps their markings. For a baby, a clear face and a simple background help. Costumes and props in the photo can confuse the app about body shape, so a plain photo of the pet usually works better than one in a full outfit; a hat is fine.

Can I put the whole family in one video?

Not in one video. The app expects one subject per photo; crop a group photo down to one person before you pick it. A family of four is four videos, each 250 coins. You can send them one after another in the group chat, or combine them in your phone's video editor if you want a single clip.

How long does it take?

A few minutes per video, from choosing the photo to a saved clip. You do not need to prepare anything in advance; you can make the video on December 24 and send it the same evening.

What happens to the family photo?

Danceify's privacy policy says this about photos: "We keep an uploaded photo only while your video is being made. It is deleted from our storage when the video is finished, fails or is cancelled — normally within minutes — and a clean-up that runs every hour deletes any photo older than six hours." It also says: "We do not use your photos or videos to train AI models." The photo is sent to the AI services that generate the video; the privacy policy names them and how long they keep files.

Two rules from the same policy apply to holiday photos in particular: "Only upload a photo of another person if they have agreed, and a photo of a child only if you are the child's parent or legal guardian."
