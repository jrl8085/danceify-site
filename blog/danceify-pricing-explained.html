Danceify Pricing Explained (November 2026)

Prices and coins · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer

Danceify - AI Dance Videos, an iPhone and iPad app, is free to download, and making a video uses coins. As of November 10, 2026, one-time coin packs start at $3.99 (US), and the Creation Weekly subscription is $11.99 a week in the US with 4,000 coins a week. There is no free trial; eligible new subscribers get the first week for $2.99 (US) with 1,200 coins, then $11.99 a week.

Key facts
  • App: Danceify - AI Dance Videos, App Store ID 6760788471. iPhone and iPad only.
  • Download: free. No account, sign-up or email; purchases go through your Apple Account.
  • Coins (◆): a Regular video uses 250 coins in app version 5.38 and later (150 in earlier versions). Premium videos cost more.
  • Coin packs: one-time, from $3.99 (US).
  • Creation Weekly: $11.99 a week (US), 4,000 coins a week.
  • Free trial: none since November 7, 2026.
  • Introductory offer: from November 10, 2026, the first week for $2.99 (US) with 1,200 coins, then $11.99 a week. One per Apple Account.
  • Earlier subscribers: people who subscribed at $9.99 keep $9.99, with 4,000 coins a week.

This article explains every price in Danceify as of November 10, 2026, in plain terms: what is free, what coins are, what the $2.99 first week is, what the App Store shows in other countries, and how to cancel. If anything here disagrees with what the App Store shows you, the App Store is right: it shows the price for your country and the terms before you confirm.

Download Danceify Free

Is Danceify free?

The download is free. You can install Danceify from the App Store, open it, browse the 17 dance styles and the preview videos, and pick a photo without paying anything. There is no account to create and no email to give; purchases go through your Apple Account.

Making a video is not free. Each video uses coins (◆), and you get coins by buying a one-time coin pack or by subscribing to Creation Weekly. There is no free video and no free trial. If you read somewhere that Danceify has a free trial, that was true before November 7, 2026; it is not true now.

What are coins, and how many does a video use?

Coins are the in-app currency. The app shows your balance, and each video takes coins from it.

  • A Regular video, one of the 17 dance styles (EDM Shuffle, Salsa, TikTok, Bollywood, Samba, Greek, Hip-hop, Ballet, Tango, Flamenco, Reggaeton, Swing, Belly Dance, Hula, Country Line, K-Pop, Irish), uses 250 coins in app version 5.38 and later. In earlier versions it uses 150 coins.
  • A Premium video, one of the themed templates, costs more coins. The app shows the cost before you create it.

Coins you buy and coins from paid subscription periods do not expire, unless Apple refunds that purchase. When a paid plan ends, your coin balance stays and the weekly refills stop. See Support for the full rules.

How much do coin packs cost?

One-time coin packs start at $3.99 (US). A coin pack is not a subscription: you pay once, the coins are added to your balance, and nothing renews. This is the option to choose if you want to make a few videos and stop.

The App Store shows the price for your country. We do not list the local prices of coin packs here, because they vary by country; open the app and the App Store shows your price before you buy.

If you specifically want to avoid a subscription, see AI dance video app without a subscription.

What is Creation Weekly?

Creation Weekly is Danceify's subscription. In the US it costs $11.99 a week and gives 4,000 coins a week. At 250 coins per Regular video, 4,000 coins cover 16 Regular videos a week. It renews automatically every week until you cancel.

People who subscribed earlier at $9.99 a week are not moved to the new price: they keep $9.99 and also get 4,000 coins a week.

What is the $2.99 first week?

From November 10, 2026, eligible new Creation Weekly subscribers get the first week for $2.99 (US) with 1,200 coins. After that first week the subscription continues at $11.99 a week with 4,000 coins a week, unless you cancel before the week ends.

Three details matter:

  • It is an introductory offer, not a free trial. You pay $2.99 on day one.
  • You get one introductory offer per Apple Account. If your Apple Account has already used one, the App Store will not show it again.
  • The App Store shows the terms and the local price before you confirm, so you can see exactly what you will pay and when.

1,200 coins is enough for 4 Regular videos at 250 coins each, with 200 coins left over that stay on your balance.

Which option should I pick?

There is no wrong answer; it depends on how many videos you want.

  • One or two videos, no commitment: a one-time coin pack from $3.99 (US).
  • A few videos this week, and you are eligible for the offer: Creation Weekly at $2.99 for the first week, with 1,200 coins (4 Regular videos). Cancel before the week ends if you do not want $11.99 a week after that.
  • Many videos every week: Creation Weekly at $11.99 a week, with 4,000 coins (16 Regular videos) a week.

How do I cancel Creation Weekly?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name at the top (your Apple Account), then Subscriptions. Find Danceify and tap Cancel. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

After you cancel, your coin balance stays and the weekly refills stop. To restore an active subscription on a new phone, open the app, go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". Refunds are handled by Apple; see Support for the steps.

What you get for the money

Every video is a 10-second dance video made from one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat, in one of the 17 dance styles. You pick a track in the app, and you can change it on the player after the video is made. Videos save to your phone and can be shared to TikTok, Instagram, Messages or anywhere else. For the steps, see How to make an AI dance video; for what a photo should look like, see photo to dance video.
