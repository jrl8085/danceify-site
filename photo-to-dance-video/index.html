Photo to Dance Video

Danceify turns one photo into a 10-second AI dance video: the person in the photo, with the same face, clothes and body, dancing a real routine with music in any of 17 styles. It takes a few minutes in the iOS app and needs no sign-up; it works for babies, dogs and cats too.

How it works

01 Pick a photo One person, standing, facing the camera, whole body head to toe in frame. The AI keeps the face, hair, skin tone and clothing exactly as in the photo.
02 Choose who is dancing and a style Pick PERSON (or BABY, DOG, CAT) and one of 17 dance styles. Each style has its own choreography and music tracks; you pick the track.
03 Get the video The 10-second video renders on Danceify's servers in a few minutes and lands in your Library. Save it to your camera roll and share it anywhere.

Questions people ask

What app turns a photo into a dance video?

Danceify, an iOS app. Upload one photo, pick a dance style, and it makes a 10-second video of that person dancing, with music. Free to download, no sign-up; videos use coins (packs from $2.99, or a weekly plan).

What photo works best?

A standing, whole-body photo of one person facing the camera, in good light. Seated poses, close-ups, group shots and screenshots give poor or no dance. A portrait (vertical) photo frames the dance best.

Does it keep my face, or is it a face swap?

It keeps you. The AI animates the person in your photo with their own face, hair, skin tone and clothing locked in every frame; it does not paste your face onto another dancer. Results vary with the photo.

How long does it take?

A few minutes. The video is made on Danceify's servers; the app shows an estimate while it renders (typically two to five minutes), and the finished video appears in your Library in the app. If a video fails to render, the coins it used are returned automatically.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

Danceify is free to download and needs no account or email. Videos use coins: a one-time coin pack costs from $2.99 and never expires, or the weekly plan refills your coins every week and can be cancelled anytime in your iPhone Settings. US-dollar prices; the App Store shows your local price.

Is there an Android or web version?

Danceify is an iOS app for iPhone. There is no Android or web version. Download it from the App Store.

Can I use it for TikTok or Instagram?

Yes. Each video is 10 seconds long, comes with a music track, and saves to your camera roll, so you can post it to TikTok, Instagram Reels or Stories, or send it in iMessage or WhatsApp. A portrait photo gives the best social-ready framing.
