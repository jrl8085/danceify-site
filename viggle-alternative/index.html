Viggle alternative for iPhone — for people, babies, dogs and cats

Updated October 27, 2026 · Written by the Danceify team. Danceify is our app.

Danceify - AI Dance Videos (App Store ID 6760788471) is an iPhone app that makes a dance video from one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat: you pick who is dancing and one of 17 dance styles, and you get a 10-second video to save to your phone and share. It is free to download, with no account, sign-up or email.
