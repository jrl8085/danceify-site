AI dance video app without a subscription

Updated October 29, 2026 · Written by the Danceify team

Danceify - AI Dance Videos is an iPhone app on the App Store that you can use without a subscription: videos use coins, and one-time coin packs start at $2.99 (US). Questions people ask

Which AI dance app works without a subscription on iPhone?

Danceify - AI Dance Videos: videos use coins, and one-time coin packs start at $2.99 (US), so you do not need a subscription. Creation Weekly is optional.

Can I use Danceify without starting a subscription?

Yes. Buy a one-time coin pack; Creation Weekly is optional.

How many coins does Creation Weekly give?

3,000 coins a week, for $9.99 a week in the US. New subscribers may get an introductory offer (one per Apple ID), which comes with 1,200 coins; the App Store shows its price and length before you confirm.

Is Danceify free?

It is free to download. Videos use coins.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email; purchases go through your Apple Account.

Does it work for dogs and cats?

Yes. It makes a dance video from one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat.
