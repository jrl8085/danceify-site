Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 3, 2026

Danceify ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: When you create an account we store your email address and a user ID to manage your account, credits, and subscription.
  • Photos you upload: When you generate a dance video, your selected photo is temporarily uploaded to our servers for processing. It is automatically deleted once the video is generated.
  • Generated videos: Dance videos are stored locally on your device. We do not keep copies on our servers.
  • Purchase history: We record which subscriptions or credit packs you have purchased so we can grant you the correct credits and features. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • Usage data: We store your total generation count and credit balance to provide the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To generate dance videos from your photos.
  • To track your credits and subscription status.
  • To improve the app experience.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): We use Firebase for authentication, database, and temporary file storage. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • MiniMax: Your uploaded photo is sent to MiniMax's API to generate the dance video. The photo is not retained by MiniMax after processing.
  • Apple StoreKit: In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

Uploaded photos are deleted immediately after video generation. Your account data (email, credits, generation count) is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing us. If you delete your account, all associated data will be removed from our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Danceify is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
