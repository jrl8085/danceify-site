Danceify - AI Dance Videos: the facts

Danceify - AI Dance Videos is an iPhone app on the App Store (App Store ID 6760788471). It turns one photo of a person, a baby, a dog or a cat into a 10-second dance video, in any of 17 dance styles, and there are also Premium themed templates. Videos save to your phone and can be shared. It is free to download, with no account, sign-up or email; purchases go through your Apple Account. Videos use coins (◆): one-time coin packs start at $2.99 (US), and Creation Weekly is $9.99 a week in the US with 3,000 coins a week, plus an introductory offer for new subscribers whose price and length the App Store shows before you confirm. The App Store shows the price for your country. Facts current as of October 28, 2026.
